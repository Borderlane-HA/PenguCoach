# Activity showcase (Alpha.76)

Every activity has a **Showcase** button next to the AI analysis. It opens a preview of a designed image of the session that you can save as PNG or share directly (mobile browsers with system sharing).

## What the card shows

- **Route** as a clean line without map tiles, keeping the real shape (north up). Start is the open circle, finish the filled one. Optionally coloured by **heart rate** or **speed**; colours spread over the range recorded in this session.
- **Headline figures** that suit the sport: distance, moving time, pace (running, walking, hiking, swimming per 100 m) or speed (cycling), elevation gain, average heart rate, power (cycling) or energy. Missing values are left out, never shown as zero.
- **Elevation profile** along the distance when altitude was recorded.
- **Heart-rate zones**: recorded zone times (SparkyFitness) or your Garmin zone profile for the sport.
- **Personal bests**: a badge when a 1-, 5- or 20-minute best effort of this session is also your best of the last year (same calculation as “My recorded best efforts”).
- Activities **without GPS** (strength, indoor) show a heart-rate, power or speed waveform instead of the route.

## Styles and formats

| Style | Look |
|---|---|
| Night | dark green, glowing route |
| Light | paper tone, framed |
| Poster | bold orange-red |

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Portrait 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 px |
| Story 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 px |
| Landscape 16:9 | 1600 × 900 px |

The chosen style, format and route colour are remembered in the browser.

## Notes

- The image is created in your browser from data PenguCoach already shows; nothing is uploaded and no database change is involved.
- Start and finish of the route are shown exactly as recorded. Keep this in mind before sharing an image publicly.
- The card uses the browser's system font; the exported PNG matches the preview on the same device.
