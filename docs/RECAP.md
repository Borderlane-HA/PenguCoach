# Activity recap / Rückblick (Alpha.77)

The **Rückblick** button in the activity journal opens an animated full-screen story about your training. Choose the period at the top: **Heute, Woche, Monat, Jahr, Gesamt**. Search term and source filter of the journal apply as well, so "Garmin" plus the search "Lauf" gives a recap of your Garmin runs.

## Slides

Slides without data are skipped.

1. **Intro** – number of activities, active days and sports.
2. **Distance** – total with a comparison (marathons, Munich → Berlin, share of the Earth's circumference) and a bar per day or month.
3. **Elevation gain** – total compared with the Eiffel Tower, Zugspitze, Mont Blanc or Mount Everest, with a filling mountain.
4. **Time and energy** – hours in motion (Tatort episodes) and kilocalories (pizzas).
5. **Your mix** – sports by training time, with count, distance and time.
6. **Highlights** – longest session, biggest climb, fastest run from 3 km, strongest ride by average power. Each card opens the activity.
7. **Your routes** – all recorded GPS routes drawn on top of each other. The view focuses on the area where most routes are; a single far-away holiday route is left out of the frame. At most the newest 300 routes are used.
8. **Consistency** – calendar (week, month, year) or monthly bars (all time), longest streak and current streak (a streak may end yesterday).
9. **Compared** – change in distance, time, elevation and activities against the same span of the previous period up to today (e.g. 1–23 October vs. 1–23 September).
10. **Closing card** – 1080 × 1350 px PNG to save or share.

## Controls

Tap or click the right part of the screen for the next slide, the left part for the previous one. Arrow keys and the buttons at the bottom work too; space or the pause button stops the timer. The recap pauses when the browser tab is hidden. With reduced motion enabled in the operating system, numbers appear without counting and routes without drawing.

## Calculation

- Periods use your time zone (profile setting); a week starts on Monday.
- Distance, elevation and energy only add values that were recorded; activities without them do not count as zero.
- Time is moving time where available, otherwise elapsed time.
- Routes are thinned to 140 points each for display.
